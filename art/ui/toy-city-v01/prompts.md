# Simple Sci-Fi UI sheet

Create ONE polished landscape UI DESIGN SHEET for 'MISSILE SIMULATOR', a Roblox city destruction collection game. Direction: VERY SIMPLE, easy to build using native Roblox Frames, UIStroke, UICorner and TextLabels. Pet Simulator 99 inspired approachable large tap targets, clear resource counters and inventory grid, but ORIGINAL design with a tougher restrained SCI-FI equipment-control character. NOT cute toy-plastic 3D UI. NOT a complex military cockpit. Flat slate/navy panels, off-white clear typography, cobalt blue selection/action accent, small amber status accents, green confirmation, red close/danger only. Small corner radius 4-6px, straight clean edges, one thin border, modest flat fills. No bevels, bolts, chrome, metallic textures, excessive glow, holographic rings, hex patterns, decoration, tiny technical labels. Only simple flat 1-2 color glyph icons will be uploaded; all other interface elements must look straightforward to construct from rectangles and text in Roblox.
Sheet composition on light gray background with clean margins: header 'MISSILE SIMULATOR / SIMPLE SCI-FI UI'. Upper left 60% width: large gameplay HUD preview on bright primary-color toy New York city (white buildings, blue trim, red awnings, green trees), small third-person block avatar on hoverboard with missiles behind. City is merely soft secondary background; keep center 70% unobstructed. Compact top-left counters 'PARTS 1,250' and 'DATA 12' with tiny flat nut and flask glyphs; compact top-right objective 'CITY 01', 'DESTROY 2 / 4'; a small target HP bar near a building; bottom center small four square navigation buttons with flat symbols and labels 'BASE', 'MISSILES', 'WORLD', 'RIDE'; bottom-right simple blue rectangular 'FOCUS [F]' button; no oversized crosshair or giant card across center.
Upper right 40%: missile inventory panel titled 'MISSILES', small red X, three simple tabs 'ALL', 'EQUIPPED', 'RARE'; 2 rows of 3 square cards, flat missile silhouettes centered, small count/rarity strip, selected card blue outline plus checkmark. Bottom one blue 'EQUIP' button. Simple clean grid, useful whitespace, all text large.
Bottom third: component sheet with clear groups. Left: four identical rectangular button samples labeled 'NORMAL', 'HOVER', 'PRESSED', 'DISABLED' with modest color changes. Center: simple HP bar, reload bar, resource chip, selected/unselected inventory slot. Right: one row of eight tiny flat monochrome glyphs: hex nut, flask, missile, home, box, globe, hoverboard, target. These glyphs are basic solid silhouettes, uniform stroke/weight, NO 3D shading. Small bottom note 'ICONS: IMAGES / PANELS + TEXT: ROBLOX'. Headings and short English labels only; no logos, no premium store. Confident sporty sci-fi, accessible mobile simulator rather than hardcore tactical clutter. Sheet must primarily show simple buildable UI, not illustration or shiny icons.
